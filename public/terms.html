coolify-mcp is an open-source Model Context Protocol server that bridges AI assistants to Coolify v4 infrastructure. Using this hosted multi-tenant deployment entails accepting the terms below.

What the service is

  • It is an API bridge and protocol translation proxy between your MCP client and your Coolify instance.
  • It does not host your applications, manage your servers, or provide computing resources. All deployment targets remain on your own Coolify platform.
  • It is provided free of charge, as-is, with no formal service level agreement (SLA). The service may experience downtime, maintenance periods, or updates without advance notice.

What is expected of you

  • Authorized access only: You may only link Coolify instances and API tokens that you own or are explicitly authorized to administer.
  • Compliance and security: You remain solely responsible for the actions initiated through your assistant, including software deployed, configuration changes made, and resources terminated.
  • No misuse: You agree not to use the server to conduct penetration testing against third parties, bypass rate limits, or disrupt the shared multi-tenant infrastructure.
  • Account security: You are responsible for keeping your GitHub or Google credentials secure. Anyone possessing access to your identity account can manage your enrolled Coolify instances.

Automated assistant actions

AI models can make errors, misinterpret logs, or execute unexpected commands. While coolify-mcp implements safeguards (such as elicitation prompts for destructive actions, secret masking, and log sanitization), you are ultimately responsible for confirming operations. Review all actions and parameters proposed by your assistant before granting execution approval.

Operator access & trust model

As documented in our Privacy Policy, this hosted service is managed by the platform operator. The operator holds administrative access to the underlying storage (HashiCorp Vault) and could technically view stored API tokens. If your security posture or regulatory requirements mandate zero-knowledge isolation or strict credential isolation from platform operators, you should run the open-source coolify-mcp server locally (via stdio) or self-host your own container.
